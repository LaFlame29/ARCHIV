About Us

ARCHIV is a curated space for discovering the ideas, objects, and spaces that shape the way we live.

From architecture and interiors to furniture, materials, and design, ARCHIV brings togheter references that deserve to be noticed. It is a place to explore different styles, discover designers, and' collect visual ideas without reducing design to a single aesthetic.

We believe thet a space is built from details. A chair, a lamp, a material, a proportion, or even a simple combinations of colors can change the way a place feels. ARCHIV exists to document these details and make them easier to discover, revisit, and connect.

More than a collection of images, ARCHIV is an evolving archive of design references - a place where inspiration can be found, compared, and turned into something personal.

Observe. Discover. Archive.
